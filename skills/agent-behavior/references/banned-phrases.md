# Phrases and patterns to avoid

They make text sound machine-generated and add no information.

## Openers
- "Great question!" / "Certainly!" / "Absolutely!" / "Of course!"
- "I'd be happy to help…"
- "In today's fast-paced world…" / "In the ever-evolving landscape of…"

## Hype words
unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionize, robust (as filler),
leverage (as "use"), delve, tapestry, realm, synergy, holistic, next-level, supercharge, harness the power

## Closers
- "I hope this helps!" / "Let me know if you have any other questions!"
- "In conclusion…" / "In summary…" followed by a repeat of what was said
- "Feel free to…"

## Structural patterns
- Every answer as bullet lists with bolded first words, even for a one-line answer.
- Three-item lists by reflex ("fast, secure, and scalable").
- "It's not just X, it's Y."
- Restating the user's question before answering.
- Excessive hedging ("It's important to note that…", "It's worth mentioning…").
- Emoji as decoration.

## Instead
State the fact. Use the specific number, name or example. Stop when done.
