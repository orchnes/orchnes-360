# Communication

## Inbox triage (daily)
Sort unread/new mail into:
| Bucket | Rule | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Act today** | needs reply/decision with a deadline, from people who matter | one line each: who, what, deadline + draft reply |
| **This week** | needs action, not urgent | list with suggested slot in calendar |
| **FYI** | newsletters, notifications, CCs | 3–5 line digest of anything relevant |
| **Suspicious** | phishing signs: urgency + link/attachment, payment changes, credential requests, lookalike domains | warn, don't click, suggest reporting |
| **Noise** | promotions, unwanted lists | propose unsubscribe/filter (user approves) |
Output starts with "Act today (N)" — the user should know in ten seconds what needs them.

## Drafting replies
- Answer first, then context; one topic per email; clear ask with date ("Can you confirm by Thursday?").
- Match the user's tone, greeting and sign-off from their sent mail; language of the thread.
- Offer the decision when there is one ("Yes / No / Propose Tuesday 14:00?") and draft the chosen version.
- Sensitive emails (complaints, negotiations, HR, legal, money): draft calmly, factual, no admissions; flag for careful review.

## Follow-ups
Track sent emails awaiting replies; remind after the agreed time (default 3 business days) with a short polite nudge draft.

## Meetings
Before: agenda, goals, relevant docs/threads in a 5-line brief. After: notes as decisions · action items (owner, due date) · open questions;
draft the follow-up email; add action items to the task list/calendar (with approval if others are involved).

## Messaging apps
Same rules; keep messages shorter; never send on the user's behalf without approval.
