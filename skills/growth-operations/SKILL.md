# Growth Operations

Growth is a system: acquire efficiently, convert well, serve fast, keep customers coming back — measured weekly.
Social media and content production live in `content-social`; analytics deep dives in `analysis`; AI support assistants in `ai-integration`.

## 1. Growth model first
Map the funnel with current numbers: visitors → product views → add-to-cart → checkout → purchase → repeat → referral.
Find the **biggest leak** (largest drop vs benchmark or vs your own history) and work there first. Unit economics guardrail from `market-research`:
don't scale acquisition while contribution per customer is negative.

## 2. Pick levers (one or two at a time)

| Conversion rate | `references/acquisition-experiments.md` | checkout friction, trust signals, page speed, A/B tests |

## 3. Operate weekly
Dashboard: traffic by channel, conversion rate, AOV, CAC by channel, repeat rate, contribution, support volume and first-response time, CSAT, refunds/returns.
Weekly review: what moved, why, next experiment (with hypothesis, metric, threshold, end date). Automate reports and reminders (`workflow-automation`).

## 4. Rules
- **Consent and law:** marketing messages only with valid consent (Türkiye İYS + 6563; EU GDPR/ePrivacy; US CAN-SPAM/TCPA) and easy unsubscribe.
- No dark patterns (fake scarcity, hidden costs, forced subscriptions, confirm-shaming) — illegal in many places and erode trust.
- One change at a time when measuring; enough sample before calling a test.
- Fix the product/service problem before amplifying it (bad reviews + more ads = faster decline).
- Everything customer-facing in the brand voice (`content-social` voice guide), human-sounding, in the customer's language.
