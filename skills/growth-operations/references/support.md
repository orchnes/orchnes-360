# Customer support operations

## Channels
Start with few, well-served channels: email/contact form + one chat channel customers already use (website chat, WhatsApp Business, Instagram DM). Publish hours and expected response times.
Marketplace messages must be answered inside the marketplace within its SLA.

## Helpdesk
One shared inbox/helpdesk (open-source options such as Zammad, Chatwoot, FreeScout; or SaaS) with: ticket per conversation, tags (order status, damage, return, product question, complaint),
customer/order context, macros (templates), SLAs, reporting. No support from personal inboxes.

## Targets (adjust to capacity)
| Metric | Target example |
|---|---|
| First response time | < 2 business hours chat/DM, < 24 h email |
| Resolution time | < 48 h for standard issues |
| CSAT | ≥ 90% |
| Contacts per order | falling over time (fix root causes) |

## Macros and knowledge base
Write macros for the top 10 contact reasons in the brand voice; personalize every use. Turn repeated questions into help-center articles
(these also feed the AI assistant's knowledge base — `ai-integration`) and product page improvements.

## Policies that prevent tickets
Clear shipping times, tracking links, care instructions, size/capacity info, breakage policy (photo + replacement), return steps — on product pages and in emails.

## Escalation
Legal threats, safety issues, press, chargebacks, VIP complaints, repeated failures → human owner immediately with context. Compensation and refunds follow written rules with approval levels.

## Learn from support
Weekly: top contact reasons, root causes, fixes shipped (product, page copy, packaging, carrier), effect on contacts per order. Support is the best free market research.
