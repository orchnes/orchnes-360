# SEO

## 1. Technical foundation (check first)
- Indexing: Google Search Console / Bing Webmaster set up; sitemap submitted; no accidental `noindex`; robots.txt sane.
- Speed and Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) on mobile; optimized images (modern formats, sizes, lazy-loading); server-side rendering for important pages.
- Clean URLs, canonical tags for variants/filters, no duplicate thin pages, HTTPS, structured data (Product, Offer, Review, BreadcrumbList, Organization, FAQ where genuine).
- Internal linking from categories to products and between related content; fix broken links and redirect chains.

## 2. On-page (products and categories)
- Keyword research by **intent** (buy: "el yapımı seramik kupa", research: "seramik kupa nasıl yapılır", gift: "hediye kupa").
- Unique titles (~50–60 chars) and meta descriptions; one H1; descriptive product copy written for people (material, size, care, story) — never copy manufacturer text across sites.
- Image alt text describing the product; original photos.
- Category pages with helpful intro text and filters that don't create indexable duplicates.

## 3. Content
Answer real questions customers ask (support tickets, review mining, "people also ask"): care guides, gift guides, behind-the-scenes, comparisons.
Quality over volume; demonstrate experience and expertise (E-E-A-T); update rather than duplicate. AI-assisted drafts must be fact-checked, specific, and edited into the brand voice.

## 4. Local SEO (if there's a physical workshop/shop)
Google Business Profile (and local equivalents such as Yandex/Apple Maps) complete with photos, hours, categories; consistent name/address/phone across directories; reviews answered.

## 5. Marketplace SEO
Titles and attributes following each marketplace's rules; complete attributes for filters; high-quality images; price competitiveness; reviews and ratings; fast shipping badges.

## 6. Measure
Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, position by page and query; organic revenue in analytics; track over months, not days.

## Never
Buying links, hidden text, keyword stuffing, doorway pages, scraped content, fake reviews — penalties and trust loss.
