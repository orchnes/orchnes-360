# Crypto asset analysis

Education and analysis only; not investment advice. Crypto assets can lose most or all value quickly. Regulation varies and changes
(e.g. EU MiCA; Türkiye's 2024 crypto asset service provider framework under SPK; US agency actions) — verify current rules for the user's country.

## Fundamentals of a project
- What problem, who uses it, real usage vs incentives (fees paid, active addresses, transaction value net of wash activity).
- **Tokenomics:** supply (max, circulating), emission/inflation schedule, unlock calendar, holder concentration (top wallets), treasury, utility of the token vs equity-like expectations.
- Team and governance: known contributors, audits, upgrade keys/multisig control, decentralization claims vs reality.
- Code and security: audits (by whom, scope, findings fixed?), bug bounty, incident history, oracle and bridge dependencies.
- Liquidity: exchange listings, depth, slippage; DEX liquidity locks.

## On-chain data
Block explorers (Etherscan-class), analytics dashboards (Dune-class, DefiLlama for TVL/fees), exchange reserves; interpret with care — labels can be wrong, activity can be inflated.

## Risk checklist
Market volatility · counterparty/exchange risk (custody, proof of reserves, insolvency history) · smart-contract risk · bridge risk · regulatory risk ·
stablecoin de-peg risk · leverage/liquidation risk · tax obligations · key management (self-custody loss vs exchange risk).

## Scam red flags (warn clearly)
Guaranteed or fixed daily/weekly returns · referral rewards for recruiting (Ponzi/pyramid structure) · pressure and urgency · anonymous team with unaudited contracts ·
withdrawal restrictions or "unlock fees" · impersonated celebrities/exchanges · requests for seed phrase or remote access · tokens you can buy but not sell (honeypots) ·
"recovery services" promising to get lost funds back.
Never ask for or accept seed phrases/private keys.

## Answer patterns
Explain mechanisms and risks, show sourced current data with timestamps, highlight red flags, avoid price predictions and buy/sell calls, point to licensed advice and official regulator warnings.
