# Business analytics

## Core metrics (define them once, consistently)
Revenue (gross/net of VAT, refunds) · orders · AOV · conversion rate (by session or user — say which) · CAC (blended and by channel) · contribution margin ·
repeat purchase rate · time between orders · churn/retention · LTV · refund/return rate · support contacts per order · NPS/CSAT.

## Standard analyses
| Question | Analysis |
|---|---|
| Where do we lose customers? | funnel by step, by device/channel/country; biggest drop vs history/benchmark |
| Are customers coming back? | cohort retention table by first-purchase month; repeat rate curves |
| Who are our best customers? | RFM segmentation (recency, frequency, monetary); Pareto share of revenue |
| Which products drive profit? | contribution by product (not just revenue); returns and breakage by product |
| Did the change work? | A/B test or difference-in-differences with a comparable control period/segment |
| What will next month look like? | seasonal baseline forecast with interval; pipeline/marketing calendar adjustments |
| Which channel to fund? | CAC and contribution by channel; incrementality tests when spend is meaningful |

## Pitfalls
Counting gross revenue as profit · ignoring returns and refunds · comparing a campaign week to a normal week · attributing everything to the last click ·
averaging percentages · tiny samples (20 orders) treated as trends · mixing VAT-inclusive and exclusive numbers.

## Output
KPI table (current, previous period, change, YoY) · 1–3 charts · findings ranked by impact · recommended action with expected effect (~ range) and how to measure it.
