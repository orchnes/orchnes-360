# Financial markets analysis

Education and analysis only. Not investment advice. In many countries personalized investment advice requires a licence (e.g. SPK-authorized institutions in Türkiye,
SEC/FINRA-registered advisers in the US, FCA in the UK) — direct users to licensed advisers for personal decisions.

## Data (time-stamp everything)
Company filings and disclosures (KAP for Borsa İstanbul, SEC EDGAR, national regulators), exchange data, central banks (TCMB, Fed, ECB), statistics offices,
fund documents (KID/prospectus, fees). Note delays: free quotes are often delayed; fundamentals lag by a quarter.

## Fundamental analysis (companies)
- Business model, revenue drivers, competition, management, risks from the annual report.
- Statements: revenue growth, gross/operating/net margins, free cash flow, debt and interest coverage, working capital, dilution.
- Valuation ratios (P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B, FCF yield) **in context**: vs own history, peers, growth and interest rates; inflation-adjusted comparisons in high-inflation economies (e.g. TL-denominated figures; IAS 29 inflation accounting).
- Scenario ranges (bear/base/bull) with explicit assumptions — not a single "target price".

## Technical indicators
Describe what they measure (trend, momentum, volatility) and their weak predictive power; never present a pattern as a forecast.

## Risk and portfolio concepts
Volatility, maximum drawdown, correlation, diversification, currency risk, liquidity, concentration, fees and taxes, leverage risk, time horizon.
Sharpe/Sortino with the risk-free rate used stated. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

## Backtesting — common traps
Look-ahead bias (using data not available at the time) · survivorship bias (delisted companies missing) · overfitting (many parameters tried, best one reported) ·
ignoring costs, slippage, taxes and liquidity · in-sample only (no out-of-sample/walk-forward test) · too few trades.
Report all variants tried, out-of-sample results, and drawdowns.

## Answer patterns
- "Should I buy X?" → explain what to evaluate (business, financials, valuation, risks, fit with goals/horizon/diversification), show the current facts with dates,
  state that the decision depends on personal circumstances, suggest a licensed adviser; no buy/sell call.
- "Will X go up?" → nobody can reliably predict short-term prices; describe drivers and scenarios with uncertainty.
