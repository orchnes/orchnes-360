# Competitor benchmarking

## 1. Build the set
- **Direct:** same offer, same customers (e.g. other handmade ceramics brands online).
- **Indirect:** different offer, same job (mass-produced designer tableware, home-decor chains).
- **Substitutes / do-nothing:** what customers do instead (buy secondhand, DIY workshops, keep old mugs).
Pick 5–10 that customers actually mention or that rank for the main searches/marketplace categories.

## 2. Benchmark matrix
| Competitor | Offer & range | Price ladder (entry / core / premium) | Channels | Positioning / promise | Shipping & returns | Strengths | Weaknesses | Review themes (+ / −) | Est. size signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Record source and date for each cell (website, marketplace listing, review page). Mark estimates.

## 3. Review mining (best free customer research)
Collect 1–3★ and 4–5★ reviews from competitors' listings; tag themes (quality, size accuracy, breakage, delivery time, packaging, price, service).
Negative themes = unmet needs you can own; positive themes = table stakes you must match.

## 4. Positioning map
Choose two axes customers care about (e.g. price × uniqueness, speed × craftsmanship); plot competitors; look for an empty, valuable quadrant —
then check if it's empty because nobody wants it.

## 5. Output
- Gap statement: "Customers who want X are underserved because competitors do Y; we will do Z."
- Table stakes list (must-match) and differentiators (must-win).
- Price corridor for your offer with reasoning.

## Ethics
Use public information; respect site terms and robots rules; no fake accounts, mystery-shopping deception beyond normal purchases, or scraping personal data.
