## Standard decisions (pick the 3 that change the path most)
- What exactly do you offer, and to whom?
- Which country/region first?
- Starting budget and expected size in year 1 (solo / small team / larger)?
- Online, physical, or both?
- Build custom vs use ready-made platforms?

## Stages
| Stage | Typical scale | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| Start | first customers | ready-made and free tools, validate demand fast |
| Grow | steady revenue | automate, own your data and customer channel |
| Scale | multiple regions/teams | custom systems, redundancy, compliance programs |

## Always human-approved
Legal filings and contracts · bank/payment accounts · tax registrations · publishing in the brand's name · spending money · granting access to accounts.
