# Sources

## Quality rubric (score each source quickly)
| Question | Strong | Weak |
|---|---|---|
| Who published it? | the authority itself (regulator, standards body, vendor docs), peer-reviewed venue | anonymous, content farm, AI-generated aggregator |
| Is it primary? | original law, data, filing, paper, changelog | summary of a summary |
| How recent? | dated, updated, matches current version | undated or superseded |
| Conflict of interest? | none or disclosed | vendor comparing itself, affiliate content |
| Verifiable? | cites data/method, reproducible | claims without method |

Confidence: **High** = primary/authoritative + current (or 2+ independent strong sources).
**Medium** = reputable secondary, or a single strong source. **Low** = community/indirect, old, or conflicting.

## Where to look by domain (examples — verify availability; prefer the official source of the region in question)
| Domain | Primary sources |
|---|---|
| Law & regulation | official gazettes and legislation portals (e.g. Resmî Gazete/mevzuat.gov.tr, EUR-Lex, national legislation sites), regulator websites, court decisions |
| Privacy | data protection authorities (KVKK board, EDPB and national DPAs, ICO), official guidelines |
| Tax & business | national revenue administrations, company registries, chambers of commerce, official e-invoicing portals |
| Health | WHO, national health ministries, clinical guidelines bodies, PubMed/peer-reviewed journals, Cochrane reviews, drug regulators (FDA, EMA, national agencies) |
| Finance & markets | exchange and regulator filings (SEC EDGAR, KAP, national exchanges), central banks, audited reports; crypto: project docs, on-chain explorers, regulator notices |
| Technology | official documentation, changelogs/release notes, RFCs and standards, source repositories and issue trackers, CVE/NVD and vendor advisories |
| Statistics & markets | national statistics offices (e.g. TÜİK, Eurostat, census bureaus), World Bank, OECD, IMF, UN data |
| Products & pricing | vendor pricing pages (time-stamp them), official availability/coverage pages |

## Search operators that save time
`site:gov.tr`, `site:europa.eu`, `filetype:pdf`, exact phrases in quotes, `-exclude`, date filters, searching the official site's own search.
