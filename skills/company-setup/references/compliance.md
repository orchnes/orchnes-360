# Compliance by region and topic

Last reviewed: 2026-11-21 — verify current law and thresholds with official sources and a qualified professional.

## Privacy / data protection
| Law | Where | Core obligations (summary) |
|---|---|---|
| **KVKK** (Law 6698) | Türkiye | privacy notice (aydınlatma metni), legal basis/explicit consent where required, VERBİS registration unless exempt, data security measures, data-subject request process, breach notification to the Board, cross-border transfer rules (revised 2024 — verify), retention/destruction policy |
| **GDPR** | EU/EEA (and anyone targeting EU residents) | lawful basis, privacy notice, records of processing (RoPA), DPO where required, DPIA for high-risk processing, processor agreements (DPA), 72-hour breach notification, data-subject rights, international transfer mechanisms |
| **UK GDPR + DPA 2018** | UK | similar to GDPR; ICO fee registration |
| **CCPA/CPRA** + other state laws | US (California and more states) | notice at collection, opt-out of sale/sharing, rights requests, thresholds apply |
| **LGPD** | Brazil | GDPR-like; DPO (encarregado) |
| **PIPL** | China | consent, localization and transfer assessments |
| **DPDP Act** | India | consent, notices, rights, rules being phased in |
| **PDPA** | Singapore, Thailand | consent, notification, DPO |
| **POPIA** | South Africa | information officer registration, conditions for processing |

## Consumer and e-commerce
- Distance-selling rules: pre-contract information, right of withdrawal (e.g. 14 days in TR and EU — verify exceptions), clear total price, order confirmation, return/refund timelines.
- Türkiye: e-commerce law (6563) — ETBİS registration, commercial electronic message consent and İYS (message management system) for marketing SMS/email, distance-sales contract and pre-information form.
- EU: Consumer Rights Directive, Omnibus price-reduction rules, Digital Services Act duties for platforms/marketplaces, Geo-blocking Regulation.
- Cookies/tracking: consent before non-essential cookies (ePrivacy/KVKK guidance); a real reject option.
- Accessibility: European Accessibility Act applies to many e-commerce services (from June 2025); check local equivalents.

## Employment (when hiring)
Written contracts, social-security registration before start, payroll and withholding, working-time rules, occupational health and safety, employee data privacy,
contractor vs employee classification.

## Sector-specific licences (examples)
Health and medical devices, pharmacy, food and food-contact materials, alcohol/tobacco, financial services/payments/crypto (licensing, AML/KYC), education,
transport/drones, cosmetics, children's products, gambling. Identify early — they can block launch.

## Minimum compliance pack for an online business
Privacy notice · cookie banner with consent log · terms of sale + distance-sales contract/pre-information · return/refund policy · imprint/company details ·
marketing consent records · data processing agreements with vendors · data retention policy · security measures (see `security-operations`) · breach response plan.
