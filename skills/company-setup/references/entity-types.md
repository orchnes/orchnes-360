# Entity types by country — examples to verify

Last reviewed: 2026-11-21. Capital minimums, fees, procedures and thresholds change — verify on the official registry/tax authority site and with a local accountant.
Figures marked ~ are indicative only.

| Country | Common types | Setup notes (verify) |
|---|---|---|
| **Türkiye** | Şahıs şirketi (sole proprietorship); Limited şirket; Anonim şirket (A.Ş.) | Ltd/A.Ş. via MERSİS + trade registry (ticaret sicili), minimum capital raised in 2024 (~50,000 TL Ltd / ~250,000 TL A.Ş. — verify current); tax office registration; SGK (social security); chamber membership; KEP address and e-Tebligat for companies; **mali müşavir** (accountant) practically required; e-Fatura/e-Arşiv obligations by threshold; e-commerce sellers register in **ETBİS**; data controllers check **VERBİS** registration duty/exemption; makers selling their own home-made goods online may qualify for **esnaf muafiyeti** (tax-exempt artisan status, revenue cap — verify with GİB), unsuitable once partners/investors are planned |
| **Germany** | Einzelunternehmen; GbR; UG (haftungsbeschränkt); GmbH; AG | GmbH share capital ~€25,000 (UG from €1); notary for GmbH/UG; trade office (Gewerbeamt); tax office registration; Transparency Register |
| **United Kingdom** | Sole trader; Partnership/LLP; Private limited company (Ltd) | Companies House online incorporation; HMRC registration (Corporation Tax, VAT above threshold, PAYE for employees); People with Significant Control register |
| **United States** | Sole proprietorship; LLC; C-Corporation (Delaware common for VC); S-Corp (tax election) | state-level formation; EIN from IRS; registered agent; state sales-tax permits; foreign founders: banking/KYC and tax-treaty questions |
| **Netherlands** | Eenmanszaak; VOF; BV | KVK registration; BV via notary; VAT number from tax authority |
| **UAE** | Mainland LLC; Free-zone company | free-zone choice by activity; visas; corporate tax regime since 2023 (verify thresholds) |
| **Estonia** | OÜ (often via e-Residency) | fully online; remote founders still face tax-residency and permanent-establishment questions at home |
| **India** | Sole proprietorship; LLP; Private Limited | MCA SPICe+ incorporation; GST registration by threshold |
| **Brazil** | MEI; ME/EPP regimes; LTDA; S.A. | CNPJ; Simples Nacional regime eligibility; NF-e e-invoicing |

## Questions to always verify
Minimum capital and when it must be paid in · who can be director/shareholder (residency rules) · notary requirement · registration timeline and fees ·
mandatory memberships (chambers) · accounting/audit thresholds · VAT registration threshold · e-invoicing obligations · beneficial-owner register.
