# Human and professional approval matrix

| Item | Agent does | Founder does | Professional |
|---|---|---|---|
| Choosing entity type | compares options, recommends | decides | accountant confirms tax impact |
| Company name | checks availability (registry, trademarks, domains) | chooses | trademark attorney for filing (`brand-ip`) |
| Articles / statutes | drafts from official templates | reviews, signs | lawyer/notary where required |
| Shareholder agreement | drafts outline and term sheet | negotiates, signs | lawyer |
| Registry filing | prepares documents and checklist | submits (e-government/notary/registry) | accountant or lawyer often files |
| Capital deposit / fees | lists amounts (~, dated) and where to pay | pays | — |
| Tax and VAT registration | prepares forms and data list | submits/approves | accountant |
| Social security / employer registration | prepares | submits | accountant/payroll |
| Bank account | prepares document list | applies, completes KYC | — |
| Licences and permits | identifies, prepares applications | submits | sector consultant/lawyer if complex |
| Privacy notice, terms, policies | drafts (marked DRAFT) | approves, publishes | lawyer/DPO review recommended |
| Registrations (e.g. VERBİS, ETBİS, ICO fee) | checks duty/exemption, prepares data | submits | DPO/lawyer if unsure |
| Contracts with vendors/customers | drafts, flags risky clauses | negotiates, signs | lawyer for high value |
| Breach notification | drafts notice, timeline | decides and sends | lawyer/DPO |
| Hiring | drafts contracts and onboarding checklist | signs | payroll/accountant, employment lawyer |

Rule: the agent never submits filings, signs, pays, or makes legal representations on the founder's behalf, even if given credentials.
