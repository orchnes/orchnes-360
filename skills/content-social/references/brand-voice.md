# Brand voice guide (template)

Build from the brand's existing writing and the founder's way of talking. One page. User signs off.

```markdown
# Voice: <Brand>

## We are / we are not
| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| warm, direct | cheesy, salesy |
| expert but plain | jargon-heavy |
| playful in small doses | sarcastic at customers |

## How we sound
- Sentence length: short; one idea per sentence.
- Person: "we" for the brand, "you" for the reader.
- Formality: <sen/siz in Turkish; tu/vous etc.> — pick one per platform and keep it.
- Emoji: max 1–2 per post, never in serious topics.
- Words we use: <...>   Words we never use: <...>

## Examples
✓ "Your order shipped this morning — tracking link is in your inbox."
✗ "We're thrilled to elevate your shopping journey! 🚀✨"

## Sensitive topics
<complaints, pricing changes, outages, social issues> → tone + who approves.
```

## Making it sound human
- Use specifics: names of products, real numbers, places, times.
- Write from the customer's situation, not the brand's excitement.
- Mix formats and openings; no repeated templates week after week.
- Read it aloud: if a person wouldn't say it, rewrite it.
