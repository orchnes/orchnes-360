# Compliance for content

Last reviewed: 2026-11-21 — verify rules for the user's country and each platform's current policies.

## Always
- **Paid partnerships / ads disclosed** using the platform's branded-content tools and the local rule
  (e.g. advertising boards' influencer guidelines in Türkiye, FTC in the US, ASA/CAP in the UK, national consumer laws in the EU).
- **AI-generated content:** follow platform labelling rules; label realistic synthetic people, voices or events.
  Check AI transparency obligations that apply (e.g. EU AI Act deepfake disclosure).
- **Copyright:** licensed music/images/fonts only; user-generated content reposted with permission and credit.
- **Personal data:** no customer data in public posts without consent; giveaway entries and DMs handled under privacy law (KVKK, GDPR…).
- **Giveaways/contests:** follow platform promotion rules and local lottery/sweepstakes law.
- **Comparative and price claims:** truthful, verifiable, current.

## Regulated categories (extra review by a qualified person before publishing)
| Category | Typical restrictions |
|---|---|
| Health, supplements, medical services | no unproven health claims, no diagnosis/cure promises; professional advertising rules for doctors/clinics |
| Finance, crypto, investing | risk warnings, no guaranteed returns, licensing rules for financial promotions |
| Alcohol, tobacco, gambling | age gating, platform bans in many regions |
| Children's products | stricter advertising and data rules |
| Legal services | professional conduct rules on advertising |

When unsure: draft, flag the specific risk, and require human approval.
