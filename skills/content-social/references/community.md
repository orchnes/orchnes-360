# Community management

## Response targets (adjust to team capacity)
| Type | Target | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Question (product, hours, shipping) | < 2 h in working hours | agent with approved facts |
| Order problem | < 2 h, move to DM | agent → support system |
| Complaint | < 1 h acknowledge | agent acknowledges, human resolves if compensation needed |
| Crisis / legal / safety / press | immediately escalate | human |
| Praise | same day | agent |

## Reply pattern
1. Use their name; reflect the specific point. 2. Answer or own the problem. 3. Next step (link, DM, ETA). 4. Sign-off in brand voice.
✓ "Ayşe, kargonun gecikmesi canını sıkmış, haklısın. Sipariş numaranı DM'den atarsan hemen kontrol ediyorum."
✗ "Değerli müşterimiz, yaşadığınız olumsuz deneyim için üzgünüz. Memnuniyetiniz bizim için önemlidir."

## Moderation
Hide/remove only: spam, scams, hate, harassment, doxxing, illegal content — per a written policy. Keep criticism visible and answer it.
Never ask for passwords, card numbers or health details in comments; move to secure channels.

## Crisis mode
Trigger: rapid negative spread, safety issue, outage, legal threat, public figure involved.
1. Pause scheduled posts. 2. Alert the human owner with links and a 3-line summary. 3. Holding reply only if pre-approved
("We're aware and looking into it; update by <time>"). 4. Log everything. 5. Post-crisis review.
