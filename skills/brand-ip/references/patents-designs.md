# Patents, utility models, designs, copyright

Last reviewed: 2026-11-21 — verify with official offices (TÜRKPATENT, EPO, EUIPO, USPTO, WIPO) and a patent attorney.

## Patents
- Requirements: **novelty** (not disclosed anywhere before filing), **inventive step**, **industrial applicability**.
- **Do not disclose before filing.** Grace periods differ: some countries allow limited grace (e.g. US 12 months for the inventor's own disclosure; Türkiye's law includes a grace period — verify scope), while Europe (EPC) generally does not. Use NDAs when discussing with manufacturers or investors.
- Routes: national application → **PCT** international application within 12 months (priority) → national/regional phases (~30/31 months from priority). US provisional applications secure a date cheaply for 12 months.
- Software and business methods: patentable only in limited forms depending on jurisdiction (technical effect in Europe); often copyright + trade secrets are the practical protection.
- Freedom-to-operate: before launching a technical product, check you aren't infringing others' patents (Espacenet, Google Patents, national databases) — attorney opinion for real risk.

## Utility models
Some countries (e.g. Türkiye — faydalı model, Germany — Gebrauchsmuster, China, Japan) offer faster, cheaper protection for technical improvements
with lower inventive-step requirements and shorter terms (e.g. 10 years). Not available in all countries (e.g. US, UK).

## Industrial designs — appearance of products
- Protects shape, lines, colours, ornament, texture of a product (very relevant for ceramics, furniture, packaging, jewellery, UI icons in some systems).
- Registered design: national (e.g. TÜRKPATENT tasarım tescili), EU registered design (EUIPO), Hague system (WIPO) for international.
- Unregistered design rights exist in some places (EU unregistered design: 3 years from first disclosure in the EU; Türkiye also recognises unregistered designs for a limited period — verify).
- Novelty and individual character required; many systems allow a 12-month grace period after the designer's own disclosure — verify before relying on it.
- Multiple designs can often be filed in one application (cost-efficient for collections).

## Copyright
- Arises automatically on creation for original works (photos, texts, illustrations, logos as artwork, software code, music).
- **Ownership:** the creator owns it unless transferred. Get written assignments from freelancers, agencies and designers (including source files);
  employment contracts should cover employee works; founders assign pre-company work to the company.
- Registration is optional in most countries but helps as evidence (in the US, registration is needed before suing and enables statutory damages).
- AI-generated material: purely machine-generated output may not be protected (e.g. US Copyright Office position); document human creative contribution.

## Choosing protection for a product
Shape/look → design registration · technical function → patent/utility model · name/logo → trademark · photos/texts → copyright with assignments ·
know-how → trade secret. Often several apply to the same product.
