# Trademarks

Last reviewed: 2026-11-21 — verify procedures, fees and deadlines on the official office websites.

## Distinctiveness spectrum (strong → weak)
Fanciful/invented ("Kodak") → arbitrary (real word, unrelated meaning: "Apple" for computers) → suggestive (hints: "Netflix") →
descriptive ("Best Ceramics" for ceramics — usually refused without acquired distinctiveness) → generic (never registrable).
Also problematic: geographic names used descriptively, common surnames, laudatory terms, shapes dictated by function.

## Nice classes
Pick classes for goods/services actually offered or planned. Example for a ceramics brand (verify wording with the office's class tool):
Class 21 (household utensils, tableware, crockery, porcelain and earthenware) · Class 35 (online retail services for those goods) ·
optionally Class 41 (workshops/courses) or Class 16 (printed matter) if relevant. Over-broad specifications invite objections and non-use attacks.

## Filing routes
| Route | Notes (verify) |
|---|---|
| National (e.g. TÜRKPATENT) | one country; foreign-domiciled applicants usually need a local attorney |
| EU trade mark (EUIPO) | one filing for all EU member states; one opposition can block it all |
| Madrid Protocol (WIPO) | international registration based on a home application/registration; designate countries; dependency on the base mark for 5 years |
| United States (USPTO) | use-based or intent-to-use; proof of use required before registration; foreign-domiciled applicants need a US attorney |
**Paris Convention priority:** file abroad within 6 months of the first filing to keep the earlier date.

## Typical process (Türkiye example — verify current details)
Application → formal examination → examination on absolute grounds → publication in the bulletin → **2-month opposition period** → registration after fee payment →
protection 10 years from filing date, renewable every 10 years. Non-use for 5 years after registration can lead to revocation (verify current rules).

## Using the mark
™ (unregistered claim) vs ® (registered — using ® for an unregistered mark can be unlawful in some countries) · consistent form · keep dated evidence of use (invoices, listings, ads).

## Watching and enforcement
Watch new applications for similar marks (office alerts or paid watch services) · oppose within the publication deadline · marketplace takedowns via brand registries ·
customs recordation for counterfeit-prone products · attorney for cease-and-desist and litigation.
