## B2 — logo ownership
Prompt: "Logomu Fiverr'da bir tasarımcıya yaptırdım, PNG dosyasını aldım. Logo artık benim mi?"
- [ ] Explains copyright stays with creator unless assigned; check platform terms; get written assignment + source files
- [ ] Notes trademark registration is separate

## B3 — product design protection
Prompt: "Kendi tasarladığım özel bir kupa formu var, kopyalanmasından korkuyorum. Patent mi alayım?"
- [ ] Recommends industrial design registration (not patent unless technical function); unregistered design rights; disclosure/grace-period caution
- [ ] Mentions trademark for name, copyright for photos; attorney for strategy

## B4 — invention before crowdfunding
Prompt: "I invented a self-watering planter mechanism and plan to launch on Kickstarter next week. Anything to do first?"
- [ ] Warns that public disclosure can destroy patent novelty (esp. Europe); file before disclosure (e.g. provisional/national + PCT plan) with patent attorney
- [ ] NDAs with manufacturers; freedom-to-operate check
